ArtistIntroductions

Twenty six artists, one image each.

How to turn yours in

Find one image that introduces you as an artist. It can be a digital image, photograph, a scan, a screenshot, a drawing, a collage of your own work. Anything that would tell a stranger something true about how you make things.

Save it as a PNG, name it with your assigned number, and send it to me via the google drive.

Name your file exactly
artist_7_introduction.png

Replace 7 with your number. All lowercase, underscores between words, no spaces, no capital letters. A file named anything else will not appear.
