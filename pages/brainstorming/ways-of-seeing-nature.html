ART 104 · Weeks 5 to 8 · Nature Machines, 3055

Ways of Seeing Nature

Fourteen lenses that different cultures and thinkers have used to see the natural world. Each card has an overview, a question, a way to try the lens on your own natural thing, and links to the readings and artworks from the course.

Deal lenses to a group

Draw three lenses, then describe your natural thing through each one for your How to see it page.

Describe your natural thing three times, once through each lens. Use one reading from each card as evidence. Then decide: which lens makes the most interesting machine for 3055, and which one does your group want to push against?

Links marked SJSU library go to the catalog search. Readings come from the Weeks 5 to 8 reading list; links under “Look further” are background sources outside the course list.
