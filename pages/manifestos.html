Manifestos

A working library of manifestos from art, technology, ecology, and feminism.

According to dictionary.com a Manifesto is "1) a public declaration of intentions, opinions, objectives, or motives, as one issued by a government, sovereign, or organization or 2) a mission statement or other document written and disseminated by an individual or group to enumerate or expound the guiding principles and beliefs that inform their actions."
