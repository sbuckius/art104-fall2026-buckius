From It to Ki · group zine

Ki Group Maker

Click the button to place artists #1 to #24 into 4 random groups. In each group, every artist becomes TeamMember 1 to 6, and each TeamMember is responsible for making one page. Three different members trace Ki 1, 2 and 3. Copy the result into Google Sheets to share it with the class.

Add student names (optional)

One name per line, in artist-number order: line 1 is artist #1, line 2 is artist #2, and so on. Leave it empty to show artist numbers only.
