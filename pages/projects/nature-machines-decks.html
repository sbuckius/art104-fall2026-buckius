Nature Machines Decks

Seven card decks for building a machine for 3055: ways of seeing nature and technology, kinds of users, ways of gathering data, the natural things and technologies from Lists A and B, and every source link from the reading list.

Deal a machine

Deal one card from each ticked deck. The group builds its machine from what it gets.

Combine the natural thing and the technology into one machine. See it through each lens you were dealt, design it for the user, and use the data method to show a sample of what it records. Open any lens card for readings and prompts. Deal again if a combination doesn't spark anything.

Links marked SJSU library go to the catalog search. Natural things and technologies link to a Wikipedia search and the SJSU catalog; items marked NEW have no source yet, and finding one is part of the work. Quotes on lens cards are from public domain sources; “Find the line” boxes point to the key passage in the course readings.
