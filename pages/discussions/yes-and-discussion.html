What you are doing

Pull one idea or one aesthetic out of the artwork above. Add it to your group's quote. Write the result as an equation, and say what the new artwork actually is: material, scale, where it lives. One sentence is enough.

Something from the artwork+The idea in the quote=A new artwork

3D modeling of plants + infrastructure can be a type of resistance = a seed library disguised as a utility box, bolted to a pole on every block.

Then the next person hits Yes and. They get one prompt, and they rewrite the whole equation with it folded in. Do not just add your bit at the end. Rebuild the sentence so it still makes sense.
