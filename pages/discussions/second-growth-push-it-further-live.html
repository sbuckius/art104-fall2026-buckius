What we heard

Before you choose, write down the reactions from the share-out. Other groups, the instructor, anything that landed or stung.

1

Pick one thing

What one part of the project do you want to push further? It can come from the notes above, or from something you already knew was unfinished.

One thing. Not three. Name it specifically enough that someone else could point at it.

2

Why this one

What is thin, unresolved, or more interesting than you first thought it was?

3

If you had more time

Describe the version with no limits. More pages, more sound, more research, a whole second issue, a physical object, a working site.

Go past what you think is doable. You will cut it back in the next box.

4

The next move

Now the small version. What is the one addition you could actually finish and drop into the zine this week?

Write it as a file: one page, one recording, one image, one link.

5

What it needs

Tools, files, permissions, people, minutes. Who does which part, and by when.

6

How you will know it worked

What would make you say the zine got better rather than just bigger? Finish the sentence: this worked if a reader…
