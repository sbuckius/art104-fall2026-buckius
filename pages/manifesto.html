Compose your plate

One plate each. It is your answer to the course question, reduced until only the claim is left. Draft it here, preview it in place, then export the block and send it to whoever is editing. Nothing is saved to a server; the export is how it travels.

Under twelve words. Present tense, no hedging, no “I think.” A manifesto line is something somebody could disagree with out loud.

Not an explanation of the image and not a summary of your reading. Say what follows if the declaration is true — what somebody would have to do differently.

A photograph, drawing, screenshot, scan, render, or a still from your video. It is not an illustration of the line. If the line already says everything the image says, change one.

Your name, a handle, or “anonymous.” Same question the Week 15 colophon asks.

Colophon

What this is. One page, made by the whole class, answering the question the course exists to answer: how can digital media art contribute to the discussion around climate justice, ecology, and sustainability? Twenty-five plates, one each, plus a preamble everybody argued over. It is the visual companion to the section manifesto written in Week 3, and it is assembled from the SPECULATE answers filed across the term.

How it gets built. Everything lives in the PREAMBLE and PLATES blocks at the top of this file’s script. Students draft a plate here and export a block; whoever is editing pastes the blocks in and commits. That is the whole pipeline. No database, no accounts, no upload. One file that will still open in ten years.

Why a manifesto and not a catalogue. A catalogue describes what was made. A manifesto says what should be the case. This course spent fifteen weeks reading manifestos that aged badly, manifestos that were co-opted, and manifestos whose authors spent decades attacking what flew their flag. Writing one at the end, collectively and in public, with everybody’s name against their own line, is the last exercise.

Set in Archivo Black, Oswald and Bitter. Printable as a two-up broadside. Back to the course site.
