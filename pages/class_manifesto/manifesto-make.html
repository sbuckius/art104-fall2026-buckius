One plate · One question

One plate each. Your answer to the course question, reduced until only the claim is left: how can digital media art contribute to conversations about climate, ecology, and nature?

Ask your editor which number is yours, so two people don't claim the same slot.

This is the manifesto. Short, sharp, arguable.

A visual for your plate. It will be saved as a PNG.

What to do with the two files

  1. Download your plate_N.txt (and plate_N.png if you added an image).
  2. Send both files to your editor, exactly as named.
  3. The editor drops them into the site's plates/ and images/plates/ folders.
  4. Your plate then appears on the manifesto page automatically.
