    The SPECULATE ladder

    The sixth question needs a shape or it turns into freewriting. Four rungs, the same every week.

    1. Name the object. One ordinary thing that fits in a hand or a room. If it cannot be photographed, it is not an object yet.
    2. Site it. Where is it, who made it, what did it cost, what did it replace? An object with no supply chain is a logo.
    3. Hand it over. Give it to somebody in a different position and say what breaks. From Week 5.
    4. Name the strategy. Which of the fourteen? Later in the term.

    When every sheet is "completed", the composing bench opens and you build the slides you will present. Then export three files. Due two hours before class.
